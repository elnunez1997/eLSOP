2 CUSTOMER GENERAL INFORMATION

Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customer’s documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsalable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820 or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included)? Yes
Sometimes the Weis customer reference number will include additional verbiage. When searching for the customer invoice, you only have to search using the numerical portion of the reference number. For example, if the customer reference number is 63823809CREDIT, you only need to search for 63823809. If the customer reference number is A/R246975-1NewStore, search only on 246975-1.
Weis customer invoice totals will generally not match the claim header amount because Weis factors in a discount amount. To verify the claim amount against the customer invoice, you need both the customer invoice and the check remit. Reference to the invoice number on the customer invoice:
Find the same customer invoice number on the check remit with the word CREDIT behind it and negative dollar amounts.
On the check remit column labeled Discount Amount, subtract the amount shown from the amount of the customer invoice.
The customer invoice amount minus the discount amount will equal the claim header amount. Proceed with validating the claim.
Keep in mind that we are responsible for different types of claims for different clients. For example, if the $1951.80 in this example includes $1393.80 in shortages, and we do not process shortages for that client, you will need to factor the difference ($558) minus the $41.29 discount amount, for an actual claim amount of $516.71.
STORE SHIPMENT RECAP billings:
Beginning in 2019, Weis will take scans as billbacks, based on shipments to the stores. This applies to all ongoing scans EXCEPT those for clearance, discontinued items, and seasonal markdowns.
For contracts prior to April 2019 that show scans but are being converted to Store Shipment Report (SSR), follow the guideline by Weis of Sunday begin date 4 days prior to the start of performance and Saturday end date 3 days after end of performance. For contracts from April 2019 forward, the dates are shown as Store Shipment Recap Dates. These are the dates that are referenced on the customer invoice.
If the submitted contract was for unit scans, it will be invoiced as SSR units. If the submitted contract was for case scans, it will be invoiced as SSR cases.
SECONDARY SUPPLY CHARGES
Per the attached policy letter, if Weis is unable to get product directly from a vendor due to out of stock, they will pull the product through C&S and deduct $1.00 per case for the product(s) that are procured from C&S. This applies only to product that was bought through C&S because the vendor couldn’t fill Weis’ order.
DBW Info:
DBW with rounding issues
Oftentimes, Weis will round total amounts. To ensure the DBW displays the correct amount, complete the case quantity fields and the total promotion amount fields.
Contract Info:
Prior to CY 2020, Weis contracts are done in Excel format, and the forms have multiple tabs. The contract information will be found on the first tab.
For CY 2020 and forward, Weis contracts are generated via Aptaris.
Weis contracts end on Friday, and their billing period runs through Saturday of the same week. If the billing end date is not more than one day after the contract end date, the contract can be used to validate the claim.
Other General Info:
POST AUDIT POLICY:
Weis’ statute of limitations for review of all contracts is four years. Acosta policy is to disallow any post audit older than two years and pursue repayment. For Weis, this becomes a policy vs policy. Work with sales on next steps.
