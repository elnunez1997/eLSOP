2 CUSTOMER GENERAL INFORMATION

Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customer’s documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsaleable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820) or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included? YES
For the Brookshire Grocery Customer, the Customer Invoice and Contract will be same for many of their deductions (claims where they have to bill the Client back for monies offered – Billbacks – Ads – Slotting). The Customer Invoice will be searched under Back UP Documentation and the Customer Contract will be available under Event Scheduler. There will be no dollar amount available to use in the Search Screen since we are using the Customer Contract as the Customer Invoice as well.
If an invoice says ‘pricing error’ it could be for many things – list cost pricing, promotional allowances or freight discrepancies.
All promotional deduction invoices reference the customer’s SAP portal processed check stub. The number in the first column is an internal number which is unnecessary to a CRA. Be sure to look at the check stub to make sure the invoice number on the claim is correct.
We do not receive actual customer invoices for billback claims, lump sum ads, slotting, missed deals or price protection. The processed (accepted/approved) SAP documents serve as invoices for these types of deductions.
With the exception of billback claims, all of these will be in the form of lump sums.
It will be necessary to back into billback claims.
Divide the amount deducted by the billback rate on the SAP deal sheet to determine the number of cases being billed.
Fair Share (retail merchandising) invoices may have a pre of “FS”.
Po related/Invoice Reduction Notifications (IRN) for payment differences is mailed to the client the same day and the customer will not provide additional backup.
Price changes require 60 days notice and approval per document:
Contract Info:
Using the date ranges found on the Customer Invoice will help to narrow down the Event Scheduler search results.
The “Deal Number” can sometimes be used in the Customer Event # field of the Search Screen to aid in finding the Customer Contract. NOTE: The Deal Number is not always loaded into this field by the sales team, so if the Customer Contract is not found by using this number in the Customer Event # field, you will need to refine the search.
All contracts are done in the customer’s web SAP portal, and when accepted, the processed SAP deal is sent to the BMA to link to the VPF.
All deals are contracted to this customer in ORDER DATES.
POP Info:
The validity dates on the customer’s processed SAP document are the order dates for the promotion, so sales analysis reports can be based on these dates.
Ads may be found in Feature Vision.
Other General Info:
If Brookshire Grocery acknowledges that a repay is due to the client, the invoice will show this repayment. The repay line will have to be split from the valid claim amount, however, this line will be closed with a note stating that Brookshire Grocery repaid on the same check as the deduction. No repay letter is needed. The check remit must be attached to the claim as proof the amount was repaid.

6 PROCESS STEPS

1. Deduction Type: Off Invoice
A per-case off invoice allowance is given to the customer as an automatic discount for product purchased from the client. If the customer does not receive the allowance off the face of the invoice they will deduct for it. If the customer requested an allowance that is not valid, the CSC will send a notification to the customer in writing. Customers view purchase orders as contracts. If they are not notified of allowance discrepancies, they will pay according to their purchase order total.

2.Deduction Type: Billback
A Billback claim is a per-case allowance offered for a specific performance requirement.
A per case billback allowance is claimed by the direct buying customer for product purchased from the client, or by the indirect customer for product purchased from the direct customer. This is an allowance the customer will bill the client for. This is not an automatic discount given off the face of the invoice.
