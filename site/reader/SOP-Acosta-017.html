2.Deduction Type: Scan
An allowance for products scanned at the store through the register and is usually billed in eaches, not cases. The customer should not be referencing a purchase order on a scan allowance.
A Scan invoice for SEG/Winn Dixie will have an added 5% scan service charge. The entire invoice including the service charge needs to be validated for the claim to be cleared.
The Service Fees shown on the customer invoice show they are calculated at 5%, however, this 5% is an ‘average’ amount and can vary up or down depending on each individual store involved. Because of this process, we need to change the method of how we load the Service Fee amount on the DBW.
Service Fees are applied to each of the Distribution Centers listed within the customer’s invoice. We will need to add up the fees for each Distribution Center shown and then calculate what ‘average’ percent will need to be loaded in the Admin Fee % column on the DBW.
To do this, add all Service Fees together, then divide that amount by the total promotional amount from the “Scan” DBW. If we use the customer invoice in Example #3 below, our calculations would be:
All Service Fees added together = $1,346.08. Total Scan amount for all items = $27,010.64. $1,346.08 divided by $27,010.64 = .0498351. Move the decimal over two spaces to get a percent of 4.98351. If we load this number in the Admin Fee % column on our input screen, the Service Fee will apply to each category involved. Because of rounding issues, we will most likely be a few pennies off the actual Service Fee total added from the customer’s invoice. That small difference should be added or subtracted from the category with the highest promotional volume on the category report.
