2 CUSTOMER GENERAL INFORMATION

Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customer’s documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsalable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820 or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included)? Yes
Invoices from Northeast Share Services, if there is a “TM” code on the invoice, customer is for Tops Market, needs to be indexed and processed with customer signon: TOPSBU and promotions.
If the invoice reflects “PC” or has no code, invoice is for Price Chopper, to be indexed and processed with customer signon: GOLUNE and promotions. In some cases, invoices may include both TM and PC. When this occurs, use the customer sign-on under which the claim originated. Ensure the correct customer sign-ons are selected in the DBW grid so the claim routes through the proper workflow.
There is a 10% scan upcharge policy in place for Price Chopper. This 10% upcharge is reflected in units scanned. The invoices will reflect the 10% upcharge fractions on the units scanned (1.1 units, 2.2 units).
Golub Finance Policy - Finance Charges on Unpaid Invoices
If the header amount of the claim is different than the customer invoice the difference could be the finance charge that C&S will apply when deducting for an unpaid check request. Review the C&S check remit to confirm they are deducting for GOLUB.
