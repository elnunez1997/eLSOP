Pre-Pay deductions
Pre-Pay claims (Invoice ex. 0001085895PP):
Pre-Pays are not deductions and will NOT have any backup documentation except for Check Remit and Client Order Invoice.
UNFI is pre-paying the client prior to receiving the PO in order to receive the cash discount. The amount will usually be 1% of the total client order invoice amount. If claim amount is not reflecting cash discount amount or 1% of total invoice amount, email deductions@unfi.com and erap@unfi.com to find out why it was short paid.
Pre-Pay details:
· PO related
· Only backup documentation available are the Check Remit, Order Invoice, and the Customer PO.
· After prepayment goes out, UNFI then compares the invoice to what was actually received and make a final payment with the corrected dollar amount after researching the shipment.
· If the prepayment is on any other date, the payment is based on the face value of the invoice then adjusted accordingly to the research UNFI conducts on the PO.

Other General Info:
United Natural Foods Inc Policies and Procedures:
United Natural Foods charges an Administration Fee for Reclaimation Invoices. These fees are listed on a separate invoice ending in DPC. No backup is provided, just the invoice number on the check remit. The policy is to charge 20% of the cost of the reclaimed products, subject to a maximum fee charge per banner per month of $250, and a minimum charge per banner of $10 per month.
UNFI will assess a 3% service level fee to supplies that do not meet the 95% fill rate of all purchase orders. They will apply the 3% only to the shored product based on the 95% fill rate. (Please see document above service level assesement fee for more information).
