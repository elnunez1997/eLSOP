BUSH:
Approval for using Request Funding from Client
If the CCA needs funds, because the CCA can request funds in the client system for the client to approve, we will use the Requested Funding From Client step which is owned by the CCA and has a 7 day follow up
If at the 7 day follow up, the CCA still has not received funding approval, we will step the claim to Request Additional Funds-Action Required, and seek BM assistance through appropriate notes on the claim line for actions we have already taken and need their help with to get the funding issue resolved.

1.Deduction Type: Off Invoice
A per case off invoice allowance is given to the customer as an automatic discount for product purchased from
the client. If the customer does not receive the allowance off the face of the invoice they will deduct for it. If the customer requested an allowance that is not valid, the CSC will send a notification to the customer in writing. Customers view purchase orders as contracts. If they are not notified of allowance discrepancies, they will pay according to their purchase order total.

2.Deduction Type: Bill Back
A Bill Back claim is a per case allowance offered for a specific performance requirement. A per case bill back allowance is claimed by the direct buying customer for product purchased from the client, or by the indirect customer for product purchased from the direct customer. This is an allowance the customer will bill the client for. This is not an automatic discount given off the face of the invoice.
