2 CUSTOMER GENERAL INFORMATION

Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customers documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsalable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820 or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included)? Yes
Other General Info:
RMG will not deduct invoices, even those past 30 days due, without first notifying Acosta or the client. Regardless of the due date the check request should be processed unless Sales notes the claim to not process.
RMG will accept partial check payments. If a portion of the check is deemed invalid, the valid portion can be paid and a invalid check request letter can be issued for the invalid portion.
If check requests for Retail marketing group are not paid in time, RMG adds a 5% fee per invoice and a $500 fee per statement.
Spin Reports will be available upon request from RMG for the Bozzuto’s movement (See Bozzuto’s SOP for additional details on Spins).
RMG changed the name of their supermarkets from Thriftway to IGA.
