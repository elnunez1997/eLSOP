3 INDIRECT INFO

Indirect Customers:
Harmons
Reams
AFS has a policy that allows indirects to have different dates than originally offered. This is to ensure that not all retailers are promoting on the same week. EXAMPLE: If the allowance is offered for 4 weeks, each retail group gets 4 weeks, but some may get a 7/2-7/27 period while a competing group may get the same promotion for 7/9-8/3. This may make a deduction invoice look like the retailer took 4 or 5 weeks, but it is just the change of promotion windows to different groups.
NOTE: The different ad groups of AFS can run their TPRs on different time periods per their ad/promotion dates in their respective regions.
The attachment below is the AFS TPR policy that explains why dates on the contract and dates on the invoice are off by 7 days.

When an Associated Food Store invoice references an indirect customer claim, the indirect customer claim is needed to validate the deduction (see example below). The indirect customer claim can be found using the Search Screen under Backup Documentation – Doc Type 2 Customer Invoice.

Deduction Type: Bill Back
A Bill Back claim is a per case allowance offered for a specific performance requirement. A per case billback allowance is claimed by the direct buying customer for product purchased from the client, or by the indirect customer for product purchased from the direct customer. This is an allowance the customer will bill the client for. This is not an automatic discount given off the face of the invoice.
