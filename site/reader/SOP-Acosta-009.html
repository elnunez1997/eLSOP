5 PROCESS STEPS

Deduction Type: Bill Back
A Bill Back claim is a per case allowance offered for a specific performance requirement. A per case bill back allowance is claimed by the direct buying customer for product purchased from the client, or by the indirect customer for product purchased from the direct customer. This is an allowance the customer will bill the client for. This is not an automatic discount given off the face of the invoice.
Bill Back Billing Period
McLane bills Bill Back invoices weekly on Thursdays. The billing period is for the previous Thursday thru Wednesday. For example, if the Date of Charge on the customer invoice is 4/10/14, McLane is billing for 4/3/14 – 4/9/14.
When we present a new Bill Back promotion to McLane and there is an existing promotion with the same data (ie. Items, amount), McLane will extend the existing promotion already in their system. The Date of Change needs to be used when validating a claim against a contract. In the below example, the Date of Charge is 3/12/14. This means McLane is billing for 3/6/14 – 3/11/14. However, the Buy Dates are shown as 2/1/12 – 3/11/14. This means that this promotion has not changed in McLane’s system since 2012, only the promotion has been extended from year to year.
As long as the contract covers the billing period, it can be used to validate the customer invoice. In the example above, the billing period is 4/3/14 – 4/9/14. As long as the contract covers this date frame, it is not required for it to cover the Buy Date of 2/1/12 – 3/11/14 due to McLane extending contracts as described above.
