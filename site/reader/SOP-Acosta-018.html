When reviewing a C&S Customer Invoice number Invoice # or the check remit, if the Invoice number begins with 1, 2 or 3 and contains 9 digits, this is a C&S deduction invoice number the last 3 digits represent the buyer code The C&S Buyer Code Listing is used as a reference tool to identify which market area is responsible for the claim/deduction.
The Buyer number List can be accessed in Acosta Relay main screen under Claims then SOP Documents and should be used to confirm which division or indirect the customer invoice is referencing, although not all indirects are listed on this job aid.
On the Buyer Number List, column B or C, all West Coast buyer numbers are identified as ‘West Coast’. All other buyer numbers belong to East Coast (if buyer number identifies as Metro use CSWMNE, all others are part of Northeast CSWHNE)
If C&S deducts on behalf of an indirect, the customer sign-on should be changed to that of the indirect. See Client or Customer SOP for Client specific exceptions.
If there is a difference between the last 3 digits of the customer invoice number and the buyer number on that same invoice, go by the buyer number. Example, invoice ends in 227 and buyer number is 647. 647 is the number to use with the C&S Buyer Number List for identification.
The majority of the C&S invoices will start with “CLA” moving forward, the buyer list was updated to reflect one tab.
CLA invoices will typically have a three digit buyer number included at the end however if the buyer number begins with a “0” the “0” will be dropped off from the invoice number. For example if the buyer number is 052 the CLA invoice will only include the CLA the invoice number and 52.
The CLA invoices will include an Owner field which will sometimes include a buyer number. If the buyer number listed here is different than the buyer number from the invoice number, the buyer number from the owner field should be considered.
If the CLA invoice doesn’t have a performance date but it has a contract number, the dates on the contract are the date to use to validate the claim.
If the CLA invoice doesn’t have a contract # or performance date then the claim needs to be step to the Sales team.
If the buyer number on the customer invoice is not found on the C&S Buyer Number List, please notify Bob Fallavolita at Bfallavollita@acosta.com in order to have the discrepancy investigated.

2. Deduction Type: P-Vendor (P&D Pass and Deduct) Billing
C&S is a direct customer and buys from the client. The C&S indirect customers "bill back" to C&S their OIs, BBs, Scans, as these indirects cannot deduct from the client.
P-Vendor deductions are deals set up at C&S for specific indirect customers. Instead of setting up the deal as a billback at the retail customer level, or an OI at C&S, the deal is set up as a P-Vendor deal. It is similar to an OI, except it is for a specific indirect customer for a specific time frame. The indirect customer buying through C&S gets invoiced by C&S at the lower net cost and C&S bills back the vendor weekly for what gets shipped to the customer. In addition to the invoice, C&S provides detailed movement information for the P-Vendor billings. These deductions are noted on the check remittance or customer invoice as P-Vendor, PVND or P&D (Pass and Deduct).
NOTE: Some indirect customer contracts may state OI, but they are billing back to C&S. As long as all other criteria match, the dollar amount, the dates, the items, it is still the correct contract. However, when the invoice AMOUNT does not match the indirect customer invoice — the C&S check remit must then be reviewed to determine if there is an additional service charge amount added for multiple invoices taken on the check remit.
