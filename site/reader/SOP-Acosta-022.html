4 CLIENT SPECIFIC VALIDATION INFORMATION

CAMPBELL’S
For check requests for CAMPBL and PLUMO: Due to the way General Trading generates invoices, claims are excessive in length in ACOSTA Relay.
Check request payments for CAMPBL and PLUMO will have the DBW created without following the PPG instructions in the client SOP. The DBW will be created by client event.
CCAs will use the e4 Allocate based on planned spend functionality to apply spend to the PPGs within the client event tactic by adding the event to the payment, placing the line amount on the Paid Amount under Event/Tactic, ensuring Planned Spend is reflected under Allocation Metric and then clicking the Allocate button. The paid amount under Funds does not need to be manually updated when following this process.

1. Deduction Type: Off Invoice
A per case off invoice allowance is given to the customer as an automatic discount for product purchased from the client. If the customer does not receive the allowance off the face of the invoice they will deduct for it. If the customer requested an allowance that is not valid, the CSC will send a notification to the customer in writing. Customers view purchase orders as contracts. If they are not notified of allowance discrepancies, they will pay according to their purchase order total.

2.Deduction Type: Billback
A Billback claim is a per case allowance offered for a specific performance requirement. A per case billback allowance is claimed by the direct buying customer for product purchased from the client, or by the indirect customer for product purchased from the direct customer. This is an allowance the customer will bill the client for. This is not an automatic discount given off the face of the invoice.
