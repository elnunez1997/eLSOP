2 CUSTOMER GENERAL INFORMATION

Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customers documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsalable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820 or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included)? Yes
For P Vendor information see the C&S SOP.
Customer reference number ending in F is for the fee that C&S charges for deducting on behalf of Tops. There is no customer invoice for the fee. See the C&S SOP for further information for processing.
Tops will list only one item from the TP contract when billing on a lump sum invoice, the TP contract # on the invoice should be reviewed as it will list all items with the breakdown.
Invoices received from Northeast Shared Services should be processed as follows: If the invoice includes a “TM” code, it is for Tops Market and should be indexed and processed using the customer sign-on “TOPSBU” along with promotions. If there is “PC” or no code listed on the invoice, it is for Price Chopper and should be indexed and processed using the customer sign-on “GOLUNE” along with promotions. Invoices may include both TM and PC. When this occurs, use the customer sign-on uder which the claim originated. Ensure the correct customer sign-ons are selected in the DBW grid so the claim routes through the proper workflow.

5. Deduction Type: Slotting
A Slotting allowance is offered to the customer to purchase an item not currently stocked. It is a kind of promotional money offered to a customer as incentive to buy a new item and create a “slot” for that product on their warehouse storage shelves. It is a Trade Deduction.
