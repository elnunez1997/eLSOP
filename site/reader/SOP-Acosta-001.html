3 Advantage Program & Warehouse List

AHOLD BILL PAYER PROGRAM (pre-paid program):
The Ahold Bill Payer Agreement (BPA) program is similar to a checking account for clients' trade spending. The client determines the types of billings they want to apply to the BPA and then deposits funds according to their forecasted spend. Once the fund is activated with the first deposit, invoices are systematically applied to the client's BPA.

Bill Payer prepaid claims are created from the customer invoice. For clients on Bill Payer, an agreement is signed between the client and AHOLD.

ADVANTAGE BILLING:
- No contract or POP is required
- No client event number will be available; claims should be sent to the client for internal clearing
- Above instructions apply if no client-specific instruction is available
- PXX refers to the period/timeframe that Ahold is billing for
- Stepped as Trade – Other

4 Contract & POP Information

CONTRACT INFO:
Acoustic DemandTec contracts for all Ahold Divisions are found by searching under customer sign-on: AHOLDMST.
Two contracts are created for Stop & Shop — one for NE and one for NY. If unsure, link the S&S NE contract to the claim.
When alerting for a missing Acoustic DemandTec/VPF Contract: Add alpha code 'AUSA' IN FRONT of the numeric portion of the Acoustic DemandTec# in the check # field (e.g., 'AUSA123456').
Stock Protection on a contract usually means Floorstock Protection. If a contract has 'Cost Difference' verbiage, Sales must clarify the exact type of offer.
Comments at the bottom of a contract can be useful for claims validation and breakdown.

POP INFO:
To determine all items for POP review, consult the contract first — Ahold lists only one item per rate on invoices for Scans and Lump Sums.

AD:
It is at the discretion of the Category Manager at AHOLD whether an ad runs, even if mentioned on the contract. If no ad is found, POP-IRI data is acceptable for all promotion types except Ad Lump Sum.
Ad dates run Saturday through Thursday.

SPIN REPORTS:
Spin reports are updated weekly and provide volume for one week based on a Saturday week ending date.
Search criteria in ACOSTA Relay:
- Client Sign-on: RPTCLIEN
- Customer Sign-on: AHOLPH
- Doc Type: POP Movement/TPR - 7
- Client Ref #: Week End Date (Saturday) MM/DD/YYYY
- Customer Ref #: RAW – Report Type (e.g., RAW – PERISHABLE)

RAW – NON PERISHABLE: Contains frozen, grocery, and refrigerated items.
RAW – PERISHABLE: Contains meat, deli, and other perishable items.

Both reports should be checked for relevant data if client data is missing. Ahold is moving from C&S to Direct Warehouses.

GCC Spin Process — 5, 7 & 10 Rule (ATB claims only):
- If spins are not available, note the line 'pending performance'
- On day 5 and day 7: if not available, note 'pending performance'
- If spin is not available on day 10: step to CRA
- Priority claims take precedence over the 5/7/10 rule
