Kroger claims are billed on a weekly basis with a Saturday week-ending date. On the invoices the week ending date will be listed above the Billing Period. Kroger’s billing cut-off is noon on Saturday. The dates being billed for are the Sunday thru noon on Saturday of the week identified by the week-ending date, as long as those dates are within the contract. For example, on a division with ad dates that run Wednesday-Tuesday, a promotion with dates 8/11-8/24 will have an invoice for week ending 8/14 that is billing for 8/11-8/14 (8/14 until noon), an invoice for week ending 8/21 for 8/15-8/21 (8/21 until noon, plus any post-noon billings from 8/14), and an invoice for week ending 8/28 for 8/22-8/24 (plus any post-noon billings from 8/21).
If there are back to back promotions and the Acoustic deal # is not available, refer to the billing period listed underneath the week-ending date to confirm which contract the claim is billing for.
Due to the way the contracts are being entered into Kroger’s system, you may see a variance between the allowance offered and the amount Kroger is taking on their deductions.  If a case rate does not divide out evenly to the number of units in a case, the allowance will reflect extended decimal places, causing a slight difference. Extended decimals will only show when math is verified, because the contract and invoice rates will match.
If a contract is offered for a $3.45 KATS allowance on a case of 16, the allowance works out to $.215625 per unit which will cause a rounding issue when converted to cases.
When Kroger bills, they will reflect the extended amount based on a “unit” rate instead of a case rate. However the bill will reflect the correct case rate for the contract.
To determine if there is a case rate vs. a unit rate discrepancy, divide the extended total by the quantity on the bill.  If you get an uneven amount (in this example $3.45766129) then this is a rounding issue.
$857.50 ÷ 248 = 3.45766129
There are instances when, due to rounding, Kroger will deduct for less than the contract rate. Ex: the contract shows a case rate of $2.48 for a 24pk item. The unit rate is $.10333. Multiplied by 24, that comes to $2.4799/case. Instead of rounding up to $2.48, Kroger will sometimes cut off the number and claim only $2.47. If this occurs, the claim can still be validated, even though the contract rate is showing a higher rate than the invoice.

SUNSWEET
Claims are cleared to the UPC level with in the Promoted Product Group (PPG). A DBW or detailed math note must be provided and claims converted from units to cases when applicable. The customer sign-on is PEYTCI, regardless of which division the claim is for.
When linking the event, two events will be sometimes be provided and when the rates of the events are added up, they will equal the rate on the customer invoice and contract. Use detailed math notes to provide the CCA with instructions as to how many cases for each UPC at what rate are cleared to each event. Do not use a DBW in this situation as the DBW entry would not match the invoice.
Ex: Invoice & Contract rates are $8.17. When the contract is linked, two events are provided – one for
$4.28 & one for $3.89. The claim will be split into two lines, one for each event. Math notes must be
provided to tell the CCA how many cases are clearing at $4.28 for each UPC and how many are clearing
at $3.89 for each UPC. Example note on the line for the $4.28 event would be: UPC 02880 44cs x $4.28,
UPC 02882 40cs x $4.28. Do not use a DBW in this situation as the DBW entry would not match the
invoice.

Kroger Clearing Process Multiple Invoice Matching
When clearing Kroger, multiple invoices must be attached to match the total claim amount. To locate all required invoices:
Attach the invoice that includes the customer reference number.
Go to Search and enter the invoice date using the same start and end date 5-11-26 to 5-11-26.
In the Customer Reference field, enter a wildcard search using the last three digits of the invoice division in this format: Wild Card XXX Wild Card.
Click Find. All invoices for that specific date and division will populate.
Select and add the invoices from the search screen. Ensure the combined total equals the claim amount, then attach them to the claim.
Step the claim as CUSTOMER INVOICE ATTACHED.

REPAYS
When Kroger deducts against an Acoustic DemandTec contract incorrectly, there will often be
multiple claims that are deemed invalid. When sales receives a repay approval request for an Acoustic
related issue, the BMA will review DemandTec for all claims related to that repay that will also be deemed
invalid. The initial repay letter will be Rejected and sales will provide a list of all claims affected. The
PRS/CRA will then re-write the repay letter using the additional spreadsheet tab to list all related claims. The
PRS/CRA will look for the remaining claims in Acosta Relay, link the same repay letter to all claims and step
them all for Awaiting Repay Approval at the same time. If any claims that are not below the client’s
tolerance are not available in Acosta Relay yet, the PRS/CRA will provide the claim information to their
supervisor. The supervisor will then work with the GCC team lead to have the GCC watch for the remaining claims and escalate them immediately for repay. The PRS/CRA will then link the repay letter and step the remaining claims once they are available. If any of the identified claims have already been cleared, the PRS/CRA will work with the CCA and/or client to reverse those claims in the client’s system and issue the repay.
Category manager and Director approval: With the exception of repays for wrong vendor, all repays for invoices with ref#s that include -A, -C, -NC, -R (for pricing only), -S, and all numeric ref#s (typically client order invoice #s for PO-related deductions) require category manager approval before Kroger will repay them. Category Manager and Director approvals are required for List Cost and Off Invoice repay requests.
When the claim is stepped to Awaiting Repay Approval, enter ‘REQ CATMGR APRVL mm/dd/yy’ in the Check Number field. The mm/dd/yy is the date the claim is being stepped to Awaiting Repay Approval. Ex: REQ CATMGR APRVL 06/29/20. NOTE: If an Acoustic deal # was in the Check Number field, overwrite it with this information.
If the repay is stepped Repay Letter Approved without category manager approval, proceed with submitting the repay in Supplier Connect and use the email that were sent to request approval to link in Supplier Connect with the repay request submittion & continue stepping through the repay/uncollectible process per standard process.
The sales team will use reports to monitor claims that require category manager approval and will continue to request it as needed.
If they receive approval, they will email it to the PRS who will link it to the claim in Acosta Relay, remove the ‘REQ CATMGR APRVL mm/dd/yy’ note from the Check Number field, and then submit the apprvoal to Supplier Connect. (If the claim has already been rejected by Kroger and is in an uncollectible step, still submit the category manager approval in Supplier Connect and update their system that it is now available.)
If the category manager approval is never received, once the claim reaches resolution, the REQ CATMGR APRVL note can be removed to enter repay settlement codes.
