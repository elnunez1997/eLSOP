2 CUSTOMER GENERAL INFORMATION

Customer Invoice Info
Is line level detail indexed on the customer’s documentation (Customer Invoice, Customer Check Request, Unsaleable/Spoils, Post Audit, EDI 812/820 or Client Deduction Notice if one of these documents is included)? YES
It is imperative that the repository and portals are searched thoroughly under all necessary signons and divisions due to Savemart charging $50 for each duplicate copy of backup that is sent.
Note: SaveMart/Food Maxx does not always supply any support documentation (Customer Invoice explaining why they are deducting) for PO Related claims: including Off Invoice, Billback, Pricing and Cash Discount. You must pull the Client Order Invoice and back into the claim.
You will need to review and follow the directions provided in the job aids listed below. These are located on Acosta Relay main screen under Claims then SOP Documents.
PO Related Claims
Pricing Deduction Job Aid
Cash Discount Job Aid
**Please review the order found under View Documents of the Review Order Screen to determine which customer the order shipped to.
If, for these invoices, you are able to validate the majority as a BB or OI, GCC should split off the part that cannot be validated and escalate for Customer Invoice. Likewise, if no part of the PO can be validated, GCC should escalate for Customer Invoice.
Food Maxx will use their original purchase order (PO) to reference bill backs. This may be used to identify the QTY and allowances the customer is deducting for and assist in locating the contract. Locate the PO in ACOSTA RELAY using the Orders/Invoices/PO search screen. Under View Documents, at the top of the PO screen, a copy of the original order may be located under the Active Documents section. The contract will be the same as the purchase order, but must be indexed as a contract in ACOSTA RELAY for use in validation.
Cross Dock items are products that Food Maxx ordered which is crossing the Save Mart dock and being loaded on a truck going to Food Maxx. The product being ordered/cross docked is NOT a stock item in the Save Mart warehouse. The contract will be a Food Maxx contract.
Invoices may be indexed under any of the customer sign ons. These should be searched for by replacing the prefix (DEAL, RDS) with the wildcard (%) on the Backup Documentation search screen.
Spoils/Unsaleables may begin with the prefix PRC or SUN. However, not all PRC invoices are for spoils. Those ending in DM or A are debit memos and copies must be requested via the backup procedures outlined in the BPS Team information section.
Save Mart will take deductions for allowances offered on products that were cut from orders. These deductions are called “shorted deals”. These allowances may be Off Invoice or Bill Back, or other types of allowances. There should be a contract for these products and allowances. SEE EXAMPLE NEXT PAGE
Save Mart will only use each DEAL # once.  The “P” at the end of DEALxxxxxP stands for Partial Payment due to not enough funds. These will result in a split deduction (more than one claim tied to the customer invoice). The Customer Invoice will not have the P included on the invoice number.
Invoice numbers ending in F are for the fee C&S charges when deducting on behalf of an indirect. The check remittance will indicate "service fee". There will be no separate invoice for reference numbers ending in 'F' and these claims should not be escalated for missing invoice. See below for more information in Other General Info.
Contract Info:
Save Mart is an Acoustic DemandTec customer. an Acoustic DemandTec Contracts/Deal Sheets have a reference number that may be used to identify which store the deduction belongs to and assist in locating the contract. These numbers are listed on the customer invoice as the Deal #. The first 3 numbers indicate the division:
710 = Save Mart
810 = Food Maxx
910 = Lucky’s
NOTE: Some contracts are generated outside of Acoustic DemandTec. These may be located under any of the sign ons listed in the box above.
When searching for the Acoustic DemandTec contract use the Deal Sheet # listed on the invoice minus the letters at the end. These letters (SSI,VAC,STK etc) should not be included when completing the search in AR or entered in the Check # field when requesing contracts to be downloaded.
Even though the last letters will not be used when searching or requesting contracts to be downloaded, these letters should match on the invoice and the contract. If the VPF and the contract is not available in AR for the correct division (SSI, VAC, etc) step to sales for the contract. Effective 9/15/24 Save Mart, Lucky, and Food Maxx are owned by C&S the contract will include the STK abbreviation.
Speed Fee contracts will not have the $6.00 fee listed on their contracts.
POP Info:
For Sales Analysis Reporting - Since SaveMart/Yosemite or FoodMaxx can purchase product direct from the client or pull product from a direct customer, the report needs to be ran using all of the following customer signons: FOOMNC, SAVENC and SPRSNC. Note – Save Mart also owns FoodMaxx & Lucky’s.
Refer to the Proof of Performance Reporting Ads job aid for searching for ads.

Refer to the C&S SOP for fee examples.

Deduction Type: Off Invoice
A per case Off Invoice allowance is given to the customer as an automatic discount for product purchased from the client. If the customer does not receive the allowance off the face of the invoice they will deduct for it. If the customer requested an allowance that is not valid, the CSC will send a notification to the customer in writing. Customers view purchase orders as contracts. If they are not notified of allowance discrepancies, they will pay according to their purchase order total.

A Bill Back claim is a per case allowance offered for a specific performance requirement. A per case bill back allowance is claimed by the direct buying customer for product purchased from the client, or by the indirect customer for product purchased from the direct customer. This is an allowance the customer will bill the client for. This is not an automatic discount given off the face of the invoice.
