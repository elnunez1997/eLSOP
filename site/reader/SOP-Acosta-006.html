2 CUSTOMER GENERAL INFORMATION

Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customer’s documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsaleable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820 or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included)? YES
Red Media and Supplier Relations are the same. The lump sum on the contract is for the entire year.
Hy-Vee customer invoices often show comments that reference “week ending”, followed by a date. When searching for the contract, the date shown as “week ending” has to be in the same week the contract ends. For example:
The contract can end any day between 6/17/2013 and 6/23/2013.
Claim reason Special Price: Hy-Vee invoices show a reason code of Special Price on many different types of claims, including billback, count/recount, slotting, display, WOW Buy or E-Buy offers. Review the contract to determine what the claim is actually for.
Customer invoice number: Many Hy-Vee customer invoice numbers begin with BILLBACK. However, this does not necessarily mean the claim is for a billback. It could also be a count/recount.
Fuel Saver claims: Hy-Vee always bases Fuel Saver claims on 20 gallons. The claim amount will be 20 times the contract rate. Example: The customer invoice states FS [product] 0.40; the contract states Fuel Saver Cents Off: $0.02, with a comment of SRP of $5.99 w/.02 Fuel Saver.

Fiscal Month customer invoices:
Rather than creating a customer invoice with a specific date range, Hy-Vee may submit bills showing Fiscal Month <month> <year>.
While their fiscal year starts in October, Hy-Vee’s fiscal months align with calendar months. In this example, they are billing for June 1, 2019 – June 30, 2019. Check the comment section of the customer invoice, as they may specify a smaller time frame (e.g. week ending 6/16/2019) within the fiscal month.
Attached are the codes Hy-Vee uses on their check remits.
Contract Info:
Once a contract has been submitted to Hy-Vee, there is no way to revise it if anything changes (usually it is that the dates are extended, but could also be that product is added, the rate changes, etc.). If a contract is found but the detail on the customer invoice does not match, check the VPF Plan Notes and/or Plan or Event Attachments for documentation supporting a deal revision.
E-Buy: Hy-Vee contracts that show E-Buy Offer Form are to be treated as a count/recount. E-Buy contracts do not show a deal amount. They show a regular Store Case Price and a Special Store Cost. The difference between the two is the E-Buy offer amount. In this example, the deal amount is $21.38 per case. ($51.38 regular price - $30.00 special cost = $21.38)
Hy-Vee customer invoices and contracts often reference “term” when referring to their billing periods. This verbiage may be in the Reference Title, or may be in the Comments section.
Hy-Vee terms are four months each and are defined as January – April (1st term), May – August (2nd term) and September – December (3rd term).
Ad Offers, Store Pre-Orders and Future Deals contracts will now be pulled for Pending, Approved and Finalized statuses, see examples of Pending status below.
Other General Info:
Hy-Vee has two warehouse divisions – Chariton, Iowa and Cherokee, Iowa. There will usually be a deduction for each division. The deductions do not always occur at the same time, but most offers are made to both Chariton and Cherokee.
Hy-Vee requires a minimum of 60 day notification for pending price increases (see attached policy).

J M SMUCKER
PO related claim deductions will always have a cash discount amount that is not on the invoice remittance.
Directions for working and researching PO-related claims:
Deduction amount minus the backup amount will be considered CASH DISCOUNT (validated by JMS DRS).
Shortages and overages will be netted and the amount sent to JMS DRS to resolve if results in a deduction amount (shortage is more that overage) – if overage is higher than shortage, see below additional direction to manage.
Remaining amount (e.g. list cost, off invoice, etc.) will be deemed PROMOTION/TRADE and researched/validated by Acosta CRA.
If the OSD portion results in an OA over our $100 threshold, once the shortage portion is resolved, the remaining amount will be transferred to Acosta to manage as PROMOTION/TRADE.
Deduction will need to be split in HR per normal rules.
If GCC is unable to locate invoice in Relay, the GCC will look for invoice remittance (not the check remittance – but a customer invoice type) in AR.
If located and does not match the claim header amount- factor in the cash discount – 2% of the PO total, there will be no backup for the cash discount portion.
If no backup is located, then check for cash discount via the Patch Report.
If the claim header is more than the cash discount, backup will then need to be requested from Hy-Vee.
Invoices with “Deduct Shortage” are OI allowances on shorted product and are to be cleared to a penalty fee.
Proof of Performance issues with FHS and Coffee items are approved to clear without Sales approval.
For claims with invoices beginning with a “1” – IRI data will likely be insufficient. Client has approved these claims for clearing with insufficient POP.
When searching for claims in HR, search by the customer reference number (external number) with an asterisk at the end. This will show if there are multiple records for the same reference number.

Deduction Type: Off Invoice (OI)
A per case off invoice allowance (OI) is given to the customer as an automatic discount for product purchased from the client. If the customer does not receive the allowance off the face of the invoice they will deduct for it. If the customer requested an allowance that is not valid, the CSC will send a notification to the customer in writing. Customers view purchase orders as contracts. If they are not notified of allowance discrepancies, they will pay according to their purchase order total.
NOTE: Hy-Vee may group missed OI on cut product claims in under freight on their invoice. The claim will need to be researched for the possible OI issue before coding it as freight.
