Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customers documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsalable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820 or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included)? No.
Customer Invoice Number will start with a “K” followed by numbers. When the deduction comes through the customer reference number on the claim will begin with a “D” followed by the same numbers (see example below). Deduction Type: Off Invoice
A per case off invoice allowance is given to the customer as an automatic discount for product purchased from the client. If the customer does not receive the allowance off the face of the invoice they will deduct for it. If the customer requested an allowance that is not valid, the CSC will send a notification to the customer in writing. Customers view purchase orders as contracts. If they are not notified of allowance discrepancies, they will pay according to their purchase order total.

This is an allowance the customer will bill the client for. This is not an automatic discount given off the face of the invoice.
If contract is listing an Indirect Customer (example: Rosauers, Harvest Foods, Yokes, etc or an Ad Group) a Sales Summary is sufficient.
