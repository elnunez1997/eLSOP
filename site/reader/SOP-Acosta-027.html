MDI Pass-Thru (Pre-Paid):
Claims created from a customer invoice are pre-paid (pass-thru) claims. This is a bucket of money set aside by the client to have money pulled from vs. the customer deducting from a check. Client event numbers are not required for these claims, even if the client requires an event number, one is not required for a pre-paid claim. Event scheduler contracts may have “PASS THRU – NO EVENT” loaded as the event.
The only time a client event number will be required is if the pre-paid fund is depleted and MDI has to deduct. If the fund is depleted then claims will create from the ATB or a client deduction notice. There will be a $$50.00/$100.00 Admin Fee deduction fee.
Due to pass thru customer invoices creating claims, it is possible a duplicate warning will prompt if the customer deducts due to depleted funds. The ATB/CDN claim needs to be stepped to escalate repay issue and noted accordingly. The CRA needs to check the pass thru statements to make sure this invoice wasn’t taken from the pass thru account. If the customer invoice did not come from the pass thru, the CRA will request that the ATB/CDN claim is stepped to the GCC to validate the claim.
CRA: If the ATB/CDN is a valid deduction (did not originally come from the pass thru) close all open customer invoice created claims.

If they are not notified of allowance discrepancies, they will pay according to their purchase order total.

Billback invoices will have a “cover” invoice with little details and a “detailed” invoice that provides the dates, items and rates. The detailed invoice is required to validate.
