1Deduction Type: Billback (BB)
A billback (BB) is a per case allowance offered for a specific performance requirement.
A per case billback allowance is claimed by the direct buying customer for product purchased from the client, or by the indirect customer for product purchased from the direct customer. This is an allowance the customer will bill the client for. This is not an automatic discount given off the face of the invoice.

2.Deduction Type: Ad
A fee for featuring a client’s products in an advertisement.
NOTE: Associated Supermarket is often offered two ads. One is for Associated stores, and the other is for Compare stores. They will be billed separately. The customer name on each ad must match with the contract and customer invoice; i.e. an Associated customer invoice, contract and POP will all show Associated; a Compare customer invoice, contract and POP will all reference Compare. There is no exception to this. An Associated ad cannot be used as POP for a Compare ad claim; a Compare ad cannot be used as POP for an Associated ad claim. Also note that there may be more than one version of the Compare ad running at the same time. Ad content may differ based on the geographic market area of the stores. If the promoted product is not found in a Compare ad copy, you must find out whether there is another version for the same dates that does include the promoted product.
Associated, Compare, Met Food, Pioneer and Metropolitan Citymarket ads are available online. The site is www.metroadconnect.com or www.asgadconnect.com .

3.Deduction Type: Slotting
A slotting allowance is offered to the customer to purchase an item not currently stocked. It is a kind of promotional money offered to a customer as incentive to buy a new item and create a “slot” for that product on their warehouse storage shelves. It is a Trade Deduction. A slotting lump sum allowance can be offered for each item and/or an off invoice can be offered as an introductory allowance for the first 30 days.
