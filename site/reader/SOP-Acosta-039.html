Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customer’s documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsaleable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820 or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included)? Yes
Wakefern invoices can only be pulled from the Wakefern proprietary system.
High Radius pulls backup from the customer’s proprietary system and searches daily by date to obtain the check remit and customer invoice details from the Wakefern portal, including a copy of the customer invoice. The backup is uploaded into the repository daily.
On certain customer invoices obtained from the Wakefern portal, the customer reference # in AR may not be listed on the customer invoice. In this case, the number is obtained from the check remit information in the customer portal.
Claims may be created in ACOSTA Relay with either the PO # or the Client Order Invoice number listed in the Customer Reference # field, depending how the information is sent in by the client. When searching for the backup, if unable to locate the documentation in AR using the customer reference number, search using the customer reference number in the client reference number field.
Some invoices may be indexed with line level details that are not displayed on the invoice but were obtained from the customer portal.
Price Plus detailed backup is being pulled by High Radius. The backup will be attached to the invoice as an embedded Excel file, so the paperclip will be used to view the document. If the Excel file is not present for Price Plus invoices, the claim needs to be stepped to GCIT to pull the detailed backup.
Wakefern uses various terms to refer to one kind of claim. Lock Net, Locked Cost, Ad Lock. And Price Lock are all the same thing; the terminology varies by department. Detail cannot be requested from Wakefern. For Grocery Wholesale invoices, invoices for Wakefern’s indirect wholesalers, no contracts are needed. On PO related invoices, if the GCC can identify the amount of an item is .7% of the Vend Price, it would be considered invalid damages/unsaleables. Campbell’s pays the .7% unsaleable allowance on every invoice. The GCC will step that portion as Invalid Other Escalate-Repay Issue regardless of the dollar amount (under tolerance would not be applied since we need to address this issue with the customer). Example:
On the DBW, enter the item, quantity, and the formula for the unsaleable amount in the rate field =Vend Price*.007, in this case =32.40*.007 which calculates as $54.43. If the DBW extended amount for the item matches the extended amount on the customer invoice, it will be coded as unsaleable and escalated as a repay issue.
Please note there may be a trade portion along with the unsaleable portion. The example below is for this scenario. There is a $1.20 off invoice along with the .7% unsaleable amount.
On the DBW, enter the item for the trade allowance as the first item, then enter the remainder amount as a second item for the unsaleable amount to confirm it is .7% of the Vend Price (enter the item, quantity, and the formula for the unsaleable amount in the rate field =Vend Price*.007, in this case =24.60*.007 which calculates as $51.66. In this example the trade portion equals $360.00 and the unsaleable portion $51.66. The total for the two items on the DBW add up to $411.66 which matches the customer invoice extended amount. The trade portion would be stepped accordingly and the unsaleable portion would be split to a separate line and escalated as a repay issue.
