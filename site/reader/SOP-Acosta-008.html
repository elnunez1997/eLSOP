2 CUSTOMER GENERAL INFORMATION

Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customer’s documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsalable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820 or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included)? Yes
A suffix of ‘SW’ at the end of an order invoice number on the check remit indicates that amount is for swell allowance.
K-VAT invoices beginning with a ‘PM’ prefix include an S or T in the Type column. The S represents spoils or scans and the T stands for tracking.
Items labeled with T are count/recount and must have a contract.
Items labeled with an S will not always have a contract because it may be for discontinued items. If no contract is located to support an item marked with an S, step as Contract Not Found. If the BM/BMA provides a contract, the S is for a scan. If a contract is not provided, the BM/BMA will provide a note that states the item has been discontinued.
If the customer invoice states Deleted/Discontinued for the respective UPC and the allowance is stated as a scan or a count/recount allowance, validate it as a markdown.
KVAT will sometimes split a customer invoice between multiple checks. If this occurs, the claim total will be less than the invoice total and there will be claims in ACOSTA Relay with the same customer reference number creating a duplicate error. Refer to the GCC Step Code Job Aid/Split Claim Job Aid on how to proceed.
Contract Info:
K-VAT contracts are loaded via Event Scheduler. Use the unit and case UPC when searching for contracts, as either UPC can be on the contract.
Promotional Calendar:
Scan contracts start on Wednesday and end on Tuesday.
Tracking contracts run from Saturday to Saturday. This results in Wednesday, Thursday and Friday of contract start date being omitted on a tracking invoice, but is picked up on Wednesday, Thursday and Friday following the contract end date. Tracking claims will end the Saturday following the end date of the contract and are valid based on customer promotional policy.
On the Contract:
Specification on the allowance rate fields:
Monthly / Roller Per Case Truck: Count/Recount (C/RC)
Monthly / Roller Per Case Scan: Scan
Per Unit Scan (Closeout): Markdown
Rates can also be located in the Personalized Offers sections of the contract.
Ad requirements for promotions:
If a contract has the Weekly Ad, Monthly Ad or Special Ad boxes marked, an ad is required as part of performance per generic help. An ad may also be mentioned in the comment section.
POP Info:
A Sales Summary from ACOSTA Relay is used to support K-VAT claims unless there are specific requirements in the client SOP for POP. If the sales summary does not provide enough sales, IRI/Neilson can be used following client and generic help POP requirements.
Ads are located under ADCLIENT/MIDMKX in backup documentation.
Other General Info:
Close-out Policy
Failure Fee
If a new item fails to perform at a rate of 6 cases per week after being in the store for 9 months, a failure fee will be charged.

Closeout Program
A Close-Out is considered to be any item that is an overall Food City deletion or an item that is de-authorized from a planogram version (example – item is removed from an 8’ version, but remains in a 12’ version).
All remaining warehouse inventory must be picked up by the manufacturer.
All Close-Out items will be billed at 50% of cost on a scan basis.
Pick-up full cases at Food City Distribution Center .
Final clean up thru Reclaim.
DSD no failure fee. Credit store at time of pick up.
