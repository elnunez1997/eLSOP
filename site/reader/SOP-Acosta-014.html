Shortage/Damage –
When the customer invoice states shortage/damage the BOL is required to confirm if the claim is for shortage or damage if we validate non-trade claims for the client. Repays –
AWG requires repay requests be a minimum of $50. Also, they will not accept repay requests for order dates older than 36 months. Repays are submitted via the online portal. The portal can be referred to as AWG Connect, AWG Vendor Relations Request System, or Service Now. See imbedded document for instructions on how to submit in the portal. The portal is accessed by going to www.awgconnect.com and following the instructions in the attached job aid or by going straight https://awg.service-now.com/. Please note, the username for the two systems is different. Your password for Service Now is initially the same password as for AWG Connect, but you will be required to change your password when you first login.
Repays must be submitted individually – multiple claims cannot be grouped together into one repay letter.
Repays for Price Chopper, Alliance (including when Allian submits deductions on behalf of other indirects), and Rouse claims are submitted by the customer manager and not AWG’s portal. Refer to the Master Claims Roster tab of the AWG and Acosta Relay Cheat Sheet document to identify the customer manager for Price Chopper, Alliance, and Rouse. When the repay is due for follow up, contact the BMA via email for a status update.
Repays for AM invoices are submitted in the portal, note the claim with the case#, and send the case# to the
BMA via email. Follow up is handled by the customer manager.
NOTE: Instructions are to enter a 4-digit or 6-digit vendor # with preceding zeros. The vendor # must always total 7 digits. Add however many zeros are needed to reach 7.
Once the repay is submitted, the portal will provide a case number. Enter the case # on the claim as it is required when following up with AWG for repay status. When the claim is due for follow up, after confirming it hasn’t been repaid yet, access the portal and select Request Case Status.
Any repay that is rejected by AWG will result in a $50 fee to the client.
When AWG responds to a repay request, an email is generated by their system. If more information is needed, the email can be replied to and it will deliver to a person.
