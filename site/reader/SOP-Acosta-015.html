2 CUSTOMER GENERAL INFORMATION

Customer Invoice Info:
Is line level detail indexed on the customers documentation (customer invoice, customer check request, unsalable/spoils, post audit, EDI 812/820 or client deduction notice if one of these documents is included)? No
Certco invoices are often included with the check remittance. If the invoice included with the check remittance does not have sufficient info or is missing pages, check for the invoice under doc type 2-Customer Invoice.
Woodman’s PO is considered the invoice for Woodman’s PO related claims, and the claim is “backed into.” If the PO is not available under Orders/Invoices/PO, search for it under Backup Documentation Doc Type 2-Customer Invoice. NOTE: The customer ref# is typically the client order invoice so the COI may need to be attached first so that the PO# needed can be pulled from the COI.
Woodman’s charges a $50 fee for scan re-prints (backup requests).
When Certco deducts for Woodman's invoices, the invoices will no longer be combined as one packet in AR. The validator will need to identify the Woodman's invoice #(s) listed on the Certco backup and link the Woodman's invoice(s) to the claim separately.
Customer Contract Information
If invoice is for a scan, contracts can be located in Event Scheduler under both: CERMMW and WOODMW.
Other General Info:
Certco cash discount policy is to take the discount on the gross dollar amount not net pay amount.
Certco cost change policy is to provide a minimum of 90 days prior to the effective order date.

6.Deduction Type: Slotting
A Slotting allowance is offered to the customer to purchase an item not currently stocked. It is a kind of promotional money offered to a customer as incentive to buy a new item and create a “slot” for that product on their warehouse storage shelves. It is a Trade Deduction.
